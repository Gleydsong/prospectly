## 2024-10-14 - Safely merging ARIA attributes in React form controls
**Learning:** When building reusable form controls (like Input, Select, Textarea) in React that support custom ARIA attributes via props AND manage internal ARIA attributes (like `aria-describedby` for error messages or `aria-invalid`), spreading `{...props}` *after* defining internal attributes can inadvertently overwrite them, leading to broken accessibility.
**Action:** Always safely merge internal ARIA attributes with user-provided ones. Apply these merged attributes explicitly *after* spreading `{...props}` to ensure they take precedence and maintain both user-defined and component-level accessibility features. Also, sanitize `useId()` output for element IDs, but do not sanitize user-provided `id` or `name` props.
